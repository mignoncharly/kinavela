# Matching

Matching is not implemented in Phase 0. Its future contract is deterministic and auditable: distance, child age overlap, culture, language, interests, availability, and explicit preferences produce a reproducible score and privacy-safe explanation.

The matching RPC must return distance buckets and shared attributes, never coordinates, street/postcode, private contact details, or child names. Blocks must remove families from discovery. AI may phrase explanations later but must not determine compatibility.
