# Community Guidelines

Version 1.0  
Effective date: 11 August 2026  
Last updated: 11 August 2026

Kinavela is a shared space for family heritage and trusted community. Participate with care, cultural humility and respect for every family's boundaries.

## Do

- Ask before sharing another person's story, image or contact details.
- Use accurate, non-invasive profile information.
- Respect language, culture, disability, religion, identity and family choices.
- Keep children's information guardian-controlled and minimal.
- Use blocks and reports when a connection or conversation feels unsafe.

## Do not

- Harass, threaten, discriminate, stalk, dox or impersonate.
- Spam, defraud, solicit money or pressure someone to meet offline.
- Publish exact addresses, private contact details or another family's data.
- Upload unlawful, hateful, exploitative or sexual child content.
- Circumvent moderation, account restrictions or another person's block.

## Reports and moderation

Use the in-product report flow for family, message, Village, event and Village-support concerns. Do not put child names, schools, exact addresses, contact details or immigration documents in support posts or replies. Safety and child-protection reports may also be sent to contact@kinavela.com. Moderators may limit visibility, remove content, preserve a minimal safety record and suspend accounts. No guaranteed moderation SLA is promised.

Appeals may be sent to contact@kinavela.com with the relevant account and action. Do not republish removed content while an appeal is pending.
