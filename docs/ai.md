# AI

AI is available only through the server-side provider abstraction introduced in Phase 13 and is never an authorization, matching, or safety decision-maker. AI work uses asynchronous idempotent jobs, explicit quotas, prompt versioning, minimal personal data, and reviewable outputs; the provider remains disabled until its credentials, transfer safeguards and controller approval are configured.

Family-story adaptations must never publish automatically. Original audio, transcription, translation, and adaptation remain separate records under guardian review.
